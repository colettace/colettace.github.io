Science. Systems. Service.

My work connects computational science with practical data engineering: from biomedical imaging and longitudinal research to cloud platforms for public-sector programs.

I’m a data and analytics consultant at Fearless in Baltimore. Alongside that primary role, I provide part-time, project-based consulting through Baltimore Quantitative, helping research teams integrate, assess, and document their data.

A foundation in research. A focus on useful systems.

Good analysis begins well before a model runs. It starts with understanding the measurements, reconciling the sources, and keeping enough context for the next person to use the result.

That principle has guided my work across engineering, biomedical research, and public-sector technology.
