Baltimore Quantitative

Scientific data stewardship and consulting: curating longitudinal research data, documenting quality, and protecting participant privacy.

Baltimore, Maryland

Baltimore Quantitative is my independent consulting business, based in Baltimore, Maryland. I help research teams make complex data consistent, documented, and ready for analysis.

Stewarding two decades of SardiNIA research data

I serve as data steward and custodian for SardiNIA’s longitudinal clinical database, covering approximately 8,000 participants, dozens of assessment streams, and more than 20 years of data collection.

The SardiNIA Project brings together NIA and Italian investigators to study genetics, aging, and age-related traits and diseases in Sardinia. My role is to preserve the study’s longitudinal record while preparing curated datasets for investigators’ research.

Bringing heterogeneous measurements together

The database includes clinical examinations, laboratory results, medical histories, medications, physical assessments, ECGs, echocardiograms, and vascular measurements. Each source has its own structure, timing, and instrument conventions.

For each update, I inventory incoming and historical deliveries, review table and field coverage, and assess whether the new material is ready to merge. I prepare the data through a documented, analyst-run workflow with quality checks before and after merging.

The work addresses practical research problems: repeated measurements, differences between ECG machines, and echocardiograms collected on dates that do not align neatly with clinic visits. I also compare each update with the prior release so changes to earlier records can be identified and reviewed.

Delivering data researchers can use

I prepare curated longitudinal datasets in analysis-ready formats, along with data descriptions, descriptive statistics, cohort and visit summaries, and quality-control documentation. I also produce data extracts and datasets tailored to particular research analyses.

These curated data products and preparations have supported downstream research published by study investigators. Researchers can begin with data organized and checked for their intended analyses, with documentation of the sources, transformations, limitations, and processing decisions.

Protecting participants’ privacy

The research data I handle are subject to the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Secure handling and respect for participants’ privacy are central to this work, alongside careful data preparation and documentation.

I maintain privacy safeguards for sensitive data as part of the operating workflow. The aim is to support research while protecting the people whose participation makes it possible.

Making each release easier to review and maintain

I have strengthened the workflow’s runbook, quality-control guidance, release-report guidance, and privacy safeguards. These materials help researchers review the work and make it easier to carry forward across update cycles.

Work with me

My consulting practice draws on experience in scientific computing, longitudinal data, and software engineering. I help teams integrate partner datasets, reconcile measurements, build repeatable quality checks, and document releases for analysis and future updates.

An engagement can begin with a defined data problem: an upcoming delivery, a difficult integration, or a workflow that needs to be easier to repeat. We can establish the scope, expected outputs, and review process together.
