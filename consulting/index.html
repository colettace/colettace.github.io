Baltimore Quantitative

Independent consulting in scientific data integration, quality assurance, research workflows, and data engineering.

Baltimore, Maryland

Baltimore Quantitative is my independent consulting business, based in Baltimore, Maryland. I help research teams make complex data consistent, documented, and ready for analysis.

Bring the data together

Scientific datasets often arrive from different partners, instruments, and collection periods. I help reconcile variable definitions, units, repeated measurements, and delivery formats so the resulting dataset preserves its meaning.

Make quality visible

Quality checks should answer practical questions: what changed, what is inconsistent, and what needs investigation? I build repeatable validation and reconciliation processes that make those questions easier to answer.

Prepare for analysis and the next release

I help teams develop reusable integration workflows, data dictionaries, descriptive summaries, and documentation. The goal is a dataset that researchers can use confidently and a process that can be maintained as new data arrives.

Work with the team

An engagement can begin with a defined data problem: an upcoming delivery, a difficult integration, or a workflow that needs to be easier to repeat. We can establish the scope, expected outputs, and review process together.

My consulting practice draws on experience in scientific computing, longitudinal data, and software engineering. My work at Fearless is described separately on the Experience page .
